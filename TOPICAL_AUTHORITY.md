# Topical Authority — iot.codev.id

## Role and boundary

This domain is a specialist knowledge and commercial-support property for organizations planning, prototyping, integrating, deploying, and maintaining Internet of Things systems. It should help Indonesian decision-makers and technical teams turn operational needs into evidence-bounded IoT decisions while keeping consultation, engineering, PCB design, application development, cloud integration, testing, deployment, and maintenance transactions on the existing `/jasa-iot` routes. It does not guarantee compatibility, security, certification, savings, medical outcomes, or product performance without an exact tested configuration and current primary evidence.

## Context used

- Homepage H1: **Jasa Buat Internet of Things Professional**. Visible claims and service labels emphasize end-to-end development, multi-platform integration, security/privacy, remote monitoring/control, analytics, and real-time data.
- Homepage application labels include smart home, parking, farming, health monitoring, waste, traffic, water, energy, retail, and security systems.
- The page sitemap contains 22 URLs, including 11 `/jasa-iot` service routes for end-to-end solutions, cloud integration, maintenance, testing, PCB design, IoT applications, integration, consulting, prototypes, and data analytics, plus company/legacy pages.
- The post sitemap contains 13 URLs: the `/berita` hub and 12 existing IoT articles covering education, tourism, home security, smart city, business productivity, privacy, Industry 4.0, smart home, industrial energy, health, and general IoT change.
- Three city sitemaps contain 489 location-swapped service URLs (200 + 200 + 89). The tracked export has 1,342 files and 1,039 HTML files, including 489 city pages and 518 `wp-json` snapshots.
- Security/privacy, health monitoring, industrial control, electrical compatibility, and protocol/version claims are deep-mode signals. They do not materially change this parent map, so lean planning continues, but publication must use current vendor/standards documentation, exact interfaces and firmware versions, real test evidence, and qualified review where safety or regulated decisions are involved.

## Ignored template noise

- The 489 `/kota/jasa-iot-<lokasi>.html` routes are geographic service templates, not distinct editorial coverage.
- The 518 `wp-json` HTML snapshots, WordPress assets/runtime paths, feeds, comments, archive pages, and legacy `about/features/testimonials/home` variants were excluded.
- Repeated metadata, bodies, regional substitutions, media assets, and service-page boilerplate were not inspected.
- Existing article titles were used only as a compact overlap check; their bodies were not treated as verified technical evidence.

## Internal-link rule

Setiap artikel menaut ke hub topiknya dan ke tahap lifecycle berikutnya: discovery → arsitektur/perangkat → konektivitas/edge/cloud → aplikasi/analitik → testing → commissioning → maintenance. Artikel use case menaut ke requirement, keamanan/privacy, dan pengujian yang relevan. Artikel compatibility atau protocol wajib menyebut konteks perangkat, versi, dan bukti uji; artikel safety/health/control wajib menaut ke batas eskalasi dan tidak mengarahkan keputusan kritis hanya dari konten umum.
